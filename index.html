🌾 பாரம்பரியம் • Heritage

The Spirit of Thai Pongal

"தை பிறந்தால் வழி பிறக்கும்!" • நன்றியுணர்வும், உழைப்பும், உறவுகளும் கொண்டாடும் நன்னாள்.

Pongal is an ancient harvest festival of Tamil Nadu and the global diaspora. It represents our deep thanksgiving to Nature, the Sun God (Surya), rain, and the cattle who nourish our agrarian way of life. In Karlsruhe, we bring our age-old culture alive with warmth, music, and food.

🔥
Day 1

Bhogi Pandigai

போகிப் பண்டிகை

Symbolizes renewal and letting go of negativity. We cleanse our homes and hearts ("பழையன கழிதலும் புதியன புகுதலும்"), welcoming auspicious fresh starts.

✨ Purification & Fresh Beginnings
☀️
Day 2 • Main Day

Surya Pongal

தைப்பொங்கல்

Freshly harvested rice, jaggery, cardamom, and milk are boiled in an earthen pot till it overflows to joyful shouts of "Pongalo Pongal!", dedicated to Surya Bhagavan.

🌾 Gratitude to Sun & Nature
🐂
Day 3

Maatu Pongal

மாட்டுப் பொங்கல்

A tribute to cattle, bulls, and farm animals that assist farmers relentlessly. Their horns are painted in bright colors and adorned with garlands.

🐄 Honoring Farmers & Cattle
🎉
Day 4

Kaanum Pongal

காணும் பொங்கல்

The day of reunion, visiting elders, strengthening ties, enjoying music, sports, and folk arts. This is the spirit we bring together in Karlsruhe!

❤️ Community & Family Bonding
⏰ நிகழ்ச்சி நிரல் • Program

Festival Schedule • 06 Feb 2027

A full day of culture, joyous laughter, soulful music, authentic food, and community celebration.

10:00 AM – 10:30 AM

Mangala Isai & Traditional Welcome

மங்கள இசை & வரவேற்பு

Auspicious start with Nadaswaram, welcoming all families and guests with Chandanam, Kumkum, and warm smiles.

🪔
🌾
10:30 AM – 11:30 AM

Traditional Pongal Cooking & Pooja

பொங்கல் வைத்தல் & சூரிய வழிபாடு

Live boiling of sweet Sakkarai Pongal in clay pots with children and families chanting "Pongalo Pongal!", followed by thanksgiving prayer.

11:30 AM – 12:30 PM

Rangoli & Kolam Showcase

வண்ணக் கோலப்போட்டி & கண்காட்சி

A colorful feast for the eyes! Traditional pulli kolams, floral rangolis, and creative designs by attendees of all ages.

🎨
🍌
12:30 PM – 02:00 PM

Grand Traditional Tamil Virundhu

பாரம்பரிய அறுசுவை தலைவாழை விருந்து

Authentic South Indian feast served on traditional banana leaves: Sakkarai Pongal, Ven Pongal, Medu Vada, Sambar, Rasam, Kootu, Poriyal, Appalam, and Payasam.

02:00 PM – 03:30 PM

Cultural Performances & Folk Dance

பரதநாட்டியம், கிராமிய நடனம் & பாட்டு

Classical Bharatanatyam, energetic Karagattam, Oyilattam, Tamil children choir, poetry recitations, and live acoustic music.

🎭
🏆
03:30 PM – 05:00 PM

Traditional Games & Felicitation

பாரம்பரிய விளையாட்டுகள் & பரிசளிப்பு

Uri Adithal (blindfolded pot breaking), musical chairs, Tamil quiz, prize distribution for kids and performers, followed by closing vote of thanks.

🌾
