🌾 பாரம்பரியம் • Heritage

The Spirit of Thai Pongal

"தை பிறந்தால் வழி பிறக்கும்!" • நன்றியுணர்வும், உழைப்பும், உறவுகளும் கொண்டாடும் நன்னாள்.

Pongal is an ancient harvest festival of Tamil Nadu and the global diaspora. It represents our deep thanksgiving to Nature, the Sun God (Surya), rain, and the cattle who nourish our agrarian way of life. In Karlsruhe, we bring our age-old culture alive with warmth, music, and food.

🔥
Day 1

போகிப் பண்டிகை

Bhogi Pandigai

Symbolizes renewal and letting go of negativity. We cleanse our homes and hearts ("பழையன கழிதலும் புதியன புகுதலும்"), welcoming auspicious fresh starts.

✨ Purification & Fresh Beginnings
☀️
Day 2 • Main Day

தைப்பொங்கல்

Surya Pongal (Thai Pongal)

Freshly harvested rice, jaggery, cardamom, and milk are boiled in an earthen pot till it overflows to joyful shouts of "Pongalo Pongal!", dedicated to Surya Bhagavan.

🌾 Gratitude to Sun & Nature
🐂
Day 3

மாட்டுப் பொங்கல்

Maatu Pongal

A tribute to cattle, bulls, and farm animals that assist farmers relentlessly. Their horns are painted in bright colors and adorned with garlands.

🐄 Honoring Farmers & Cattle
🎉
Day 4

காணும் பொங்கல்

Kaanum Pongal

The day of reunion, visiting elders, strengthening ties, enjoying music, sports, and folk arts. This is the spirit we bring together in Karlsruhe!

❤️ Community & Family Bonding

Transit & Directions

Plan your journey by public transit or car to the celebration venue.

🚆

By S-Bahn / Public Transit

Take S-Bahn S7 or S8 from Karlsruhe Hauptbahnhof towards Rastatt/Baden-Baden. Get off at Durmersheim Bahnhof (approx. 12 minutes). Local bus or short walk connects directly to Würmersheim.

🚗

By Car / Highway B36

Just ~15 minutes drive from central Karlsruhe via B36 (South direction). Follow signs to Durmersheim-Würmersheim.

🅿️

Parking • வாகன நிறுத்துமிடம்

Limited parking spaces available at the site. Ample street parking is conveniently available along the surrounding neighborhood streets.
